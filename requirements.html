WEST AFRICA

Travelling Within ECOWAS?

Citizens of ECOWAS member states may benefit from regional free-movement arrangements when travelling between participating member states. However, travellers must still meet applicable immigration, identity and health requirements.

01

Your Passport Matters

Your nationality helps determine which entry arrangements apply to your journey.

02

Regional Travel

ECOWAS citizens can benefit from regional free-movement arrangements for qualifying travel within the Community.

03

Border Requirements

Travellers should still carry valid travel documents and comply with applicable border and immigration procedures.

04

Health Requirements

Health certificates or vaccination documentation may still be required depending on the journey and destination.

IMPORTANT

Travel Requirements Can Change

Entry, immigration, health and travel requirements can change without notice. Information shown on this page is intended for travel-planning purposes and does not guarantee visa issuance, entry or admission to any destination.

Requirements can differ depending on your nationality, passport type, travel purpose, length of stay, previous travel history, transit points and individual circumstances.

Always confirm the latest requirements with the relevant immigration, embassy, government or health authorities before travelling.
