WHEN TO GO

The Right Season
for Your Journey.

Kenya is particularly strong from July to October, when the Great Migration creates one of the region's most remarkable wildlife spectacles.

January to March is also a strong period for experiencing Kenya, depending on the style of journey and experiences selected.
