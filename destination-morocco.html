WHEN TO GO

The Right Season
for Your Journey.

March to May is a strong period for exploring Morocco, with generally comfortable conditions across many destinations.

September to November offers another favourable period for experiencing Marrakech, the mountains and desert landscapes.

TRAVEL REQUIREMENTS

Your Passport
Matters.

Entry requirements for Morocco depend on your nationality and travel circumstances. Requirements can change, so your exact passport situation should be verified before booking.

Many nationalities can visit Morocco without a visa for short tourism stays, while requirements for African passport holders vary by nationality.
