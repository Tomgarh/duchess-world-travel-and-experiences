SOUTH AFRICA

City Sophistication.
Wild Adventure.

South Africa brings together some of Africa's most distinctive experiences in one journey — from the energy of Cape Town to the beauty of the Cape Winelands and the quiet luxury of a private safari lodge.

Designed for travellers who want variety without compromising on comfort, this journey combines culture, scenery, food, wine and wildlife in one carefully planned escape.

THE SIGNATURE EXPERIENCE

Three worlds.
One journey.

From Cape Town's coastline to the Winelands and beyond into the wild, every part of the journey is designed to feel effortless.

01

Cape Town Arrival

Settle into a five-star Cape Town stay and experience the city at an elevated pace, with time to explore its coastline, neighbourhoods and iconic landscapes.

02

Cape Peninsula

Discover the dramatic Cape Peninsula on a private day designed around some of the region's most memorable coastal scenery.

03

Cape Winelands

Spend a day among the vineyards of Franschhoek and Stellenbosch, combining beautiful scenery with a refined food and wine experience.

04

Private Safari

Finish the journey with three nights at a luxury safari lodge, with private wildlife experiences and time to slow down in the African wilderness.

TRAVEL REQUIREMENTS

Know before
you go.

South Africa's entry requirements depend on your nationality, passport and travel circumstances.

Visa and entry rules can differ significantly between travellers. Some nationalities may be visa-exempt for short tourism stays, while others may require a visa or eVisa.

African passport holders should verify the exact requirements for their nationality before booking.
