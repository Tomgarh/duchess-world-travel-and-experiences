WHEN TO GO

Egypt in
Its Ideal Season.

Egypt is particularly appealing from October to April, when cooler conditions make sightseeing across Cairo, Giza, Luxor and the Nile more comfortable.

Your ideal travel period can also depend on the itinerary, accommodation and experiences selected.

TRAVEL REQUIREMENTS

Your Passport
Matters.

Egypt's entry requirements depend on your nationality, passport and travel circumstances.

Some nationalities may be eligible for an eVisa, while others may need to complete additional visa or consular procedures before travelling.

Requirements can change, so the exact rules for your passport should always be verified before booking and again before travel.
