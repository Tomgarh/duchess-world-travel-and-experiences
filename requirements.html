TRAVEL WITH CONFIDENCE

Prepare Before You Travel

International travel involves more than booking a flight. Your passport, destination requirements, supporting documents and travel plans all need to be considered before departure.

01

Valid Passport

Make sure your passport is valid for the required period beyond your intended arrival date and has sufficient blank pages where applicable.

02

Visa & Entry Requirements

Visa and entry requirements depend on your passport nationality, destination, travel purpose and other circumstances.

03

Supporting Documents

Depending on your journey, you may need accommodation details, travel itinerary, proof of purpose and other supporting documentation.

04

Financial Evidence

Some destinations may require evidence that you can support yourself financially during your trip.

05

Travel Insurance

Travel insurance may be required or strongly recommended depending on your destination and travel arrangements.

06

Travel Itinerary

Keep your flight, accommodation and other relevant travel arrangements organised and accessible throughout your journey.

BEFORE YOU TRAVEL

Start With Your Passport

Select the passport you will use for your journey to Egypt. Requirements can vary by nationality, so checking the correct information is an important first step.

NIGERIAN PASSPORT

Travelling to Egypt with a Nigerian Passport

Nigerian passport holders should confirm the current visa and entry process with the appropriate Egyptian diplomatic authority before making final travel arrangements.

Passport validity Your passport should meet the validity requirements applicable to your trip.
Visa requirements Confirm the applicable visa route, application process and current requirements before travelling.
Supporting documents Prepare your travel itinerary, accommodation information and other supporting documents that may be requested.
Check before booking Entry requirements and processing procedures can change. Confirm the latest information before making non-refundable arrangements.
UNITED STATES PASSPORT

Travelling to Egypt with a U.S. Passport

U.S. passport holders travelling to Egypt for tourism should confirm the current visa requirements and available application route before departure.

Passport validity Your passport should generally be valid for at least six months from the date of arrival.
Tourist visa A tourist visa is required. Depending on the circumstances, eligible travellers may use an approved electronic visa or another permitted visa route.
Travel documents Keep your passport, travel itinerary, accommodation details and visa documentation accessible.
Confirm current rules Visa fees, entry procedures and requirements may change, so verify current information before travel.
UNITED KINGDOM PASSPORT

Travelling to Egypt with a UK Passport

UK passport holders should check the current Egyptian visa and entry requirements that apply to their planned visit.

Passport validity Ensure your passport meets the validity requirements for entry.
Visa route Eligible UK travellers may have access to an electronic visa route. Confirm current eligibility before applying.
Supporting documents Prepare your itinerary, accommodation details and other documents relevant to your trip.
Verify before departure Always check the latest official requirements before travelling.
EUROPEAN PASSPORT

Travelling to Egypt with a European Passport

Visa and entry requirements are determined by the specific nationality of the passport holder. European countries do not all have identical requirements.

Check your exact nationality Confirm the requirements for the specific passport you will use.
Electronic visa eligibility Some European nationalities may be eligible for Egypt's electronic visa system.
Passport validity Ensure your passport meets the applicable validity requirements.
Confirm current requirements Check the latest official information before making final travel arrangements.
OTHER PASSPORT

Check Your Egypt Entry Requirements

Requirements vary according to nationality, passport type and travel circumstances. Select the correct passport information and confirm the applicable requirements before travelling.

Passport Confirm your passport validity and condition before travel.
Visa requirements Check whether you require a visa and which application route applies to your nationality.
Supporting documents Prepare your itinerary, accommodation details and any other documents that may be required.

IMPORTANT

Requirements Can Change

Travel and immigration requirements may change without notice. The information on this page is provided as general travel-planning guidance and should not be treated as a guarantee of visa issuance or entry.

Always confirm the latest requirements with the relevant Egyptian authorities before travelling. Duchess World Travel & Experiences can assist you with organising your travel plans and preparing the appropriate documentation.
