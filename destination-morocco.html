TRAVEL REQUIREMENTS

Know before
you go.

Morocco's entry requirements depend on your nationality, passport and travel circumstances.

Many travellers can visit Morocco without a visa for short tourism stays, while requirements for African passport holders vary significantly by nationality.

Always verify the exact entry requirements for your passport before making travel arrangements.
