WHEN TO GO

The Right Season
for Your Journey.

Seychelles can be enjoyed throughout much of the year, with different periods offering different combinations of weather, sea conditions and island experiences.

April to May and October to November can be particularly appealing for travellers looking for a balance of warm weather and favourable conditions.
