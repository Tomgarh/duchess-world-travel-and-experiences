TRAVEL WITH CONFIDENCE

Prepare Before You Travel

International travel involves more than booking a flight. Your passport, destination requirements, supporting documents and travel plans all need to be considered before departure.

01

Valid Passport

Make sure your passport meets the validity requirements for your destination and has sufficient blank pages where applicable.

02

Visa & Entry Requirements

Requirements depend on your passport nationality, destination, travel purpose and other circumstances.

03

Supporting Documents

Depending on your journey, you may need accommodation details, travel itinerary, proof of purpose and other documentation.

04

Financial Evidence

Some destinations may require evidence that you can support yourself financially during your trip.

05

Travel Insurance

Travel insurance may be required or strongly recommended depending on your destination and travel arrangements.

06

Travel Itinerary

Keep your flight, accommodation and other relevant travel arrangements organised and accessible throughout your journey.

IMPORTANT

Requirements Can Change

Travel and immigration requirements may change without notice. The information on this page is provided as general travel-planning guidance and should not be treated as a guarantee of visa issuance or entry.

Always confirm the latest requirements with the relevant authorities for your destination before travelling. Duchess World Travel & Experiences can assist you with organising your travel plans and preparing the appropriate documentation.
